Privacy policy

recipe-box is a small personal recipe app run by a private individual for family and friends. This page says what it does with your information.

What it stores

  • Your Google account name and email, only to sign you in and keep your recipes apart from everyone else's. The app asks Google for nothing else.
  • Your recipes, categories and photos, in your own account in a database hosted by Supabase. Each person can read only their own.
  • On your phone, a copy of your recipes so the app works offline.

What it sends elsewhere

  • When you use the AI features (importing a recipe from a caption, web page, screenshots or video, asking the assistant, converting measurements), the text, pictures or video you chose are sent to an AI provider (Google Gemini, and as a backup OpenRouter) to produce the recipe. Nothing is sent unless you press the button.
  • Stock photo searches send the words you typed to Pixabay.
  • Reading a web page link is done by the app's server, which opens that page for you.

What it does not do

No ads, no tracking, no selling or sharing of your data with anyone else.

Deleting your data

You can delete any recipe in the app. To remove your whole account and everything in it, ask the person who runs the app and it will be deleted.
