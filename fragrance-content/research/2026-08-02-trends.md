# Fragrance Trend Check — 2026-08-02

## What's trending right now
- **Lattafa Khamrah** (cinnamon, vanilla, dates, amber) keeps dominating PerfumeTok — warm/gourmand, budget-friendly, high compliment-rate.
- **"Cheap perfumes that smell expensive"** format is recurring every few weeks — e.g. Choco Musk (white chocolate + vanilla musk in a small 50ml bottle) goes viral under this framing.
- **Dupe-vs-original comparisons** ($30 bottle next to a $300 one) remain a top-performing format; niche/indie brands are leaning into being called "the $30 alternative to [hyped niche scent]" as a discovery hook.
- **Three dominant scent directions in 2026 discourse**: hyper-realistic fruit notes, "comforting iced latte" gourmands, and skin-like/intimate musks.
- **Boozy gourmands** (whisky, rum, cognac) and **tea notes** (matcha) are trending upward.
- **"Smellmaxxing"** — a men's-grooming-adjacent niche where creators share tips for smelling fresh/musky/clean — has its own growing audience.

## Content angles to use this week
1. **"Cheap perfumes that smell expensive" batch** — pick 2-3 budget scents (Khamrah-adjacent or Choco Musk-style gourmands) and film a reaction/first-impression comparison. High format-fit, low production cost.
2. **Dupe comparison** — pair a hyped niche scent from your `collection.md` against a budget alternative; walk through notes, longevity, and price. This is the single best-performing format right now.
3. **"Smellmaxxing" tips post** — a quick-hit listicle (blog or Reel) of layering/application tips for projection and freshness; taps a growing, underserved audience.
4. **Boozy gourmand or matcha-note spotlight** — if you own or can source one, a first-impressions video framed around the "trending direction" angle performs well while the trend is still rising.
